💡 Custom Notes Feature

The "Add Custom Note to Active Deck" card allows you to quickly create and append your own flashcards directly into the deck you currently have open, without needing to go back to the official Anki desktop app.

  • Front: Type the question or fill-in-the-blank cloze sentence (e.g. {{c1::answer}}).
  • Back: Type the corresponding answer or explanation.
  • Instant Integration: New cards are added directly to your active review queue and saved securely in your browser's local storage.
